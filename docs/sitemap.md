# Sitemap

Current routing and page structure for [canicopet.com](https://canicopet.com).

## Live Pages

- `/` — Homepage: hero carousel, value propositions, featured collection, and factory story.
- `/products` — Full product catalog with dual-view mode (Product Models vs. All Variations).
- `/products/litter-mat` — Interactive product detail page for the Cat Litter Collecting Mat (49 variants, 3 sizes, swatch picker).
- `/products/scratching-pad` — Product detail page for the Couch-Arm Scratching Pad.
- `/wholesale` — B2B supply, customization capabilities (prints, dimensions, packaging), and direct factory inquiries.
- `/about` — Factory background and manufacturing story at Aktunç Tekstil in Ankara since 1968.
- `/contact` — Direct contact channels (WhatsApp, email, phone) and general inquiries.
- `/privacy` — Privacy policy and cookie transparency.
- `/imprint` — Legal notice / Impressum.
- `/404` — Custom 404 error page.

## Internationalization

- **English (EN)**: Default language.
- **Turkish (TR)**: Client-side language toggle via `.lang-tr` class, stored user preference, and browser language detection.

## Future / Planned Pages

- `/blog` — Pet care, maintenance guides, and factory textile articles for SEO.
- `/press` — Brand kit, high-resolution media downloads, and press releases.
